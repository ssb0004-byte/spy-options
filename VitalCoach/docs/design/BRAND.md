# Brand: VitalCoach Health

- **Name:** VitalCoach Health. App display name stays "VitalCoach".
- **Lockup:** mark + "VitalCoach" + spaced "HEALTH" (`VitalBrandLockup`).
- **Logo concepts** (`docs/design/logos/`):
  - A, interlocking VC (recommended)
  - B, abstract V (leaves)
  - C, negative-space tile
- **Icon** (`docs/design/icons/`): light, dark and tinted 1024 px PNGs, without alpha. Not yet installed in the asset catalog.
- **Parent-brand attribution** ("A PrecisionPeptidePens company") appears only in:
  - the onboarding footer
  - About
  - legal pages
  - the optional splash

  It never appears next to coaching, research, compound insights or the substance catalog. Content must not imply that PrecisionPeptidePens products are recommended or favoured. The catalog and research engine contain no product or vendor listings.
