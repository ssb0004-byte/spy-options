  2. App icon: approve the light, dark and tinted set, or pick one of the alternates.

Brand

Three VC monogram concepts

A · Interlocking VC Recommended

The V passes over the C at the top and under it on the right: coach and person working together. Two strokes stay legible down to 16 pt and work in one color. It is drawn in code (VitalLogoMark), so it stays sharp at any size.

B · Abstract V

Two leaves form a V with a figure above, suggesting growth and wellbeing. Softer and friendlier, but there is no C, so it relies on the wordmark.

C · Negative space

V and C are cut out of a rounded tile. Bold and very recognisable as an app-like badge, but the thin cut-lines blur below about 24 pt.

Lockup: mark + “VitalCoach” + spaced “HEALTH”. The line “A PrecisionPeptidePens company” appears only in the onboarding footer, About, legal pages and the optional splash. It never appears next to coaching, research or product content.
