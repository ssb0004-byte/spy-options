# Items needing regulatory, legal or clinical review before public release

VitalCoach is positioned as an **educational wellness and self-tracking app**. Several planned features move closer to regulated territory. Get qualified advice on each item below; this list is not legal advice.

| Feature | Why it needs review | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Medication/supplement logging and reminders (Phase 1) | Generally a wellness or administrative function, but wording must not imply treatment management. Confirm the FDA "general wellness" and medication-adherence positioning. | Regulatory counsel |
| Interaction and contraindication alerts (Phase 3) | Can be clinical decision support. Requires a licensed, validated interaction source; review against FDA CDS guidance. Never show "no interactions found" as reassurance. | Regulatory + clinical pharmacist |
| AI coach discussing prescription drugs, hormones, peptides, experimental compounds (Phase 2–3) | Risk of being treated as individualized medical advice, or as promotion of unapproved drugs. Needs guardrails, refusal policies for dosing protocols, and a clinical safety review of prompts and outputs. | Clinical safety reviewer, counsel |
| Urgent-symptom screening | The Phase 1 phrase list is conservative but **not clinically validated**. Replace it with a clinically reviewed rule set and test it. | Clinician |
| Body-composition explanations | Avoid accuracy claims for consumer BIA. Keep the "estimate" labelling. | Clinical reviewer |
| Lab results and trend analysis (Phase 4) | Reference ranges vary with age, sex, lab and method. Avoid diagnostic language. | Clinician, counsel |
| Privacy / consumer health data | Washington MHMDA and similar state laws, the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, GDPR/UK GDPR if available in the EU or UK, and COPPA (the 18+ gate is an attestation only). | Privacy counsel |
| HIPAA | Not a covered entity today. Do **not** claim HIPAA compliance. Revisit if partnering with clinicians or insurers. | Counsel |
| App Store | Guideline 1.4.1 (medical apps; may need to disclose methodology and regulatory status), 5.1.1 (data collection), 5.1.3 (HealthKit and health research), and the privacy manifest and nutrition label. | Founder + counsel |
| Age gate | Attestation plus an optional date-of-birth check. Decide whether stronger verification is needed. | Counsel |
